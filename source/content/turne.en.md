The <strong>Extremal Combinatorics Outreach Tour</strong> is a travelling initiative to promote the field in Brazilian regions without established research groups, planned to begin in 2027.
</div>

### The Project

The tour aims to visit **3 states per year** without researchers working in extremal combinatorics. On each visit, our team delivers a four- to five-day introductory mini-course focusing on the foundations and central problems of the field.

To provide participants with teaching and support, each visit will involve:

- **2 faculty members** specialising in the field.
- **2 graduate students** to help with practical activities and work with participants.

The project is expected to run for **at least 3 years**, bringing these mini-courses to almost **10 states**. At the end of this period, we will evaluate whether to continue and expand the initiative.
