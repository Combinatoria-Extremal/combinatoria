**Dates:** January 18–29, 2027  
**Location:** IMPA, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

## Description

The **2nd Friends in Brazilian Extremal Combinatorics (FBEC)** will bring together faculty working in Extremal Combinatorics and graduate students for two weeks of collaborative work on open problems in Extremal Combinatorics. The event will also include discussions of the community’s future events, books and research projects.

Most of the programme will be devoted to work in small groups, formed around the problems and interests presented at the start of the meeting. Brief talks, where useful to the discussions, and progress reports will allow participants to exchange ideas and adjust the direction of their work over the two weeks.

## Preliminary programme

The meeting will consist of two weeks of collaborative research, with extended blocks of group work and joint sessions to share problems, obstacles and progress. The programme may be adjusted to participants’ interests.
