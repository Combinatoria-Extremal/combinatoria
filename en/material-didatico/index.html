Books#

Basic Graph Theory (Purchase)
B. Bollobás, R. Morris, 2026
Cambridge University Press.

This book offers an accessible, modern introduction to graph theory, from classical foundations such as matchings and colouring to active research areas including extremal and random graphs. With its focus on elegant proofs and essential techniques, it is ideal for students entering the field.

Combinatória (Download)
F. Botler, M. Collares, T. Martins, W. Mendonça, R. Morris, G. O. Mota, 2021
(Winner of the 2023 Elon Lages Lima Prize — SBM and SBMAC)


This two-part book ranges from an introduction to combinatorics to advanced research topics. Part I presents basic techniques in extremal combinatorics and Ramsey theory, with detailed discussions of the probabilistic method and random graphs suitable for undergraduate and graduate courses. Part II develops these topics and introduces modern research tools, including the Lovász Local Lemma, dependent random choice, Janson’s inequalities, the regularity method and the container method.

Extremal and probabilistic combinatorics (Download)
R. Morris, R. Imbuzeiro Oliveira, 2011
XXVIII Brazilian Mathematical Colloquium (SBM/IMPA). Videos

Written to accompany a course at the Brazilian Mathematical Colloquium, this text introduces central tools in probabilistic and extremal combinatorics. Random graphs and the probabilistic method are discussed in detail, providing a foundation for understanding classical and contemporary results.

Tópicos em Combinatória Contemporânea (Download)
(Purchase) C. G. Moreira, Y. Kohayakawa, 2010
Publicações Matemáticas, IMPA.

Aimed at undergraduates and newcomers to the field, this text introduces modern combinatorics through classical results. It covers extremal set theory, Euclidean Ramsey theory, and applications of algebraic and probabilistic methods, emphasising connections with other areas of mathematics.

Uma Introdução Sucinta à Teoria dos Grafos (Download)
P. Feofiloff, Y. Kohayakawa, Y. Wakabayashi

An introductory book for undergraduate students. It explores fundamental concepts such as matchings, colouring and planar graphs, connecting graph theory with linear algebra and probability.
See also the book website

Lecture Notes#

The method of hypergraph containers
J. Balogh, R. Morris, W. Samotij
Proc. ICM, Rio de Janeiro, 2018.

This article presents the hypergraph container method, a technique with a wide range of applications for controlling the structure of independent sets in hypergraphs. Applications include counting problems and results transferring extremal properties to sparse settings.

The regularity method and blow-up lemmas for sparse graphs
Y. Kohayakawa, 2016
Notes from a course at the São Paulo School of Advanced Science on Algorithms, Combinatorics and Optimization
This course introduces the regularity method and classical applications of the blow-up lemma. After reviewing the foundations of the regularity method, it discusses the blow-up lemma and its classical applications in detail. It then explores the regularity method and blow-up lemma results in the sparse setting.

Courses - Video lectures#

Uma introdução à Combinatória Extremal - 5 lectures
Fábio Botler, Maurício Collares, Taísa Martins, Walner Mendonça, Guilherme Mota

An introductory course at the Brazilian Mathematical Colloquium covering fundamental results such as Turán’s and Ramsey’s theorems. The lectures survey the field, discuss the existence and structure of forbidden subgraphs, and introduce key concepts for beginners.

The Erdős–Hajnal conjecture - 2nd EBC - 4 lectures
Alex Scott

A lecture series on the Erdős–Hajnal conjecture, which connects forbidden induced subgraphs with the existence of large cliques or independent sets. Alex Scott discusses the state of the art and recent progress on this central problem in structural graph theory.

The Probabilistic Method - 2nd EBC - 4 lectures
Rob Morris

A mini-course on the probabilistic method, one of the most powerful tools in modern combinatorics. The lectures show how randomness can prove the existence of complex mathematical structures, from basic principles to more sophisticated applications.

Extremal and Probabilistic Combinatorics - 14 lectures
Rob Morris

An in-depth course exploring the intersection of extremal and probabilistic combinatorics. Topics include Szemerédi regularity, the Lovász Local Lemma and properties of random graphs, connecting classical theory with recent developments.

Ramsey Theory and Random Graphs - 5 lectures
Rob Morris

An advanced course on Ramsey theory and random graph models. The lectures discuss research topics such as the evolution of random graphs and bounds on Ramsey numbers, using refined probabilistic techniques to solve deterministic problems.
