1st FBEC — Friends in Brazilian Extremal Combinatorics

Dates: February 2–14, 2026
Location: IMPA, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Description#

The 1st Friends in Brazilian Extremal Combinatorics (FBEC) took place at IMPA during the summer of 2026. The main focus of the event was twofold: to bring together Brazilian researchers to work on important open problems in Extremal Combinatorics; and to discuss and organise future events in Brazil, books in the area and research projects.

For the research part, the first day was dedicated to the presentation of problems and formation of groups. The following days were devoted to group work on the chosen problems, with progress reports on Friday mornings.

Brief report#

On the organisation side, Mendonça and Mota finalised with the IMPA staff the details for the 2nd WBC. Together with Araújo, Campos, Moreira and Morris, they also started organising the 3rd EBC, at UFPE, then scheduled for February 18–22, 2027. The updated dates are available on the school’s page.

The participants worked in small groups on different open problems in Extremal Combinatorics. There were four groups, with some participants belonging to more than one group, working on the following problems:

  1. Given a probability p, determine the threshold q̂ such that G(n, p) ∪ G(n, q) is Ramsey for K4.
  2. Determine the threshold for families of important graphs H for the anti-Ramsey property: every proper edge-colouring of the random graph contains a copy of H whose edges all have different colours.
  3. Determine the Ramsey number of the double star S(m, 2m), the graph formed by two stars with m and 2m edges, respectively, whose centres are adjacent.
  4. Determine the threshold for a general Ramsey property for (Kr, Ks, Kt). We write G → (Kr, Ks, Kt) if every edge-colouring of G contains a monochromatic copy of Kr, a rainbow copy of Ks or a lexicographic copy of Kt.
