2nd FBEC — Friends in Brazilian Extremal Combinatorics

Dates: January 18–29, 2027
Location: IMPA, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

Description#

The 2nd Friends in Brazilian Extremal Combinatorics (FBEC) will bring researchers together for two weeks of collaborative work on open problems in Extremal Combinatorics. The meeting will also provide an opportunity to discuss future events, books and research projects within the Brazilian community.

Most of the programme will be devoted to work in small groups, formed around the problems and interests presented at the start of the meeting. Brief talks, where useful to the discussions, and progress reports will allow participants to exchange ideas and adjust the direction of their work over the two weeks.

Organisers#

  • Guilherme Mota (USP)
  • Taísa Martins (UFF)
  • Marcelo Campos (IMPA)

Participants#

The list of participants will be announced later.

Preliminary programme#

The programme below may be adjusted to reflect participants’ interests and the progress of the discussions. All times are in Brasília time (UTC−3), with a lunch break between the morning and afternoon sessions.

Week 1 — January 18–22#

Monday, January 18

  • 10:00–12:30: welcome, brief presentations of open problems and formation of working groups.
  • 14:00–16:00: initial group discussions and setting objectives for the week.

Tuesday to Thursday, January 19–21

  • 10:00–12:30: group work.
  • 14:00–16:00: group work and exchanges of ideas among participants. Short background talks may be arranged where they directly support the problems under discussion.

Friday, January 22

  • 10:00–12:30: short progress reports and a joint discussion of obstacles and possible approaches.
  • 14:00–16:00: group work and planning for the second week, with opportunities to reorganise the groups.

January 23–24: no scheduled scientific sessions.

Week 2 — January 25–29#

Monday, January 25

  • 10:00–12:30: a brief review of objectives, followed by continued group work.
  • 14:00–16:00: group work.

Tuesday and Wednesday, January 26–27

  • 10:00–12:30: group work.
  • 14:00–16:00: group work, with discussions between groups as needed.

Thursday, January 28

  • 10:00–12:30: group work and consolidation of arguments and shared notes.
  • 14:00–16:00: continued work and preparation of a summary of progress, obstacles and open questions.

Friday, January 29

  • 10:00–12:30: final reports and discussion of results, partial progress and open questions.
  • 14:00–16:00: an optional session to plan continued collaborations and discuss future events, books and research projects.

Groups will be encouraged to keep shared notes distinguishing proved results, partial arguments and conjectures. Final reports may present partial progress and new questions; a complete solution is not required.
