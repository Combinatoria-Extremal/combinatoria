UFPE · RECIFE, PERNAMBUCO

3rd Brazilian School
of Combinatorics

February 22–26, 2027

Programme Flyer (PDF) ↓

The 3rd Brazilian School of Combinatorics (EBC) will take place in person at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), in Recife, Brazil. The programme includes two mini-courses, eleven plenary talks, poster presentations and sessions devoted to open problems.

The school welcomes undergraduate and graduate students, postdoctoral fellows and researchers from Brazil and abroad. Plenty of time will be set aside for working on problems in small groups.
