The 3rd Brazilian School of Combinatorics (EBC) will take place in person at the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), in Recife, Brazil. The programme includes two mini-courses, around ten plenary talks, poster presentations and sessions devoted to open problems.

The school welcomes undergraduate and graduate students, postdoctoral fellows and researchers from Brazil and abroad. Plenty of time will be set aside for working on problems in small groups.
